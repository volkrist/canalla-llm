Harden the paid path for a second live attempt

The stream reader now treats its read timeout as a stall detector bounded by
the run's remaining budget, and the LIVE backend gets a 120-second first-line
budget instead of the product's 20-second chat budget, so a cold model load
cannot abort the first generation.

The grant records when it was claimed, keeps that timestamp when the Pod is
confirmed, and can never be un-used after confirmation. An unknown provider
answer is reconciled before anything else. A genuinely new owner decision is
recorded with `authorize.py --replace-consumed`, which archives the consumed
record; the monitor re-arms itself for a new unused grant and stays disarmed
for the old one.

The watchdog is given the runner's pid and stops an orphaned Pod after a
30-second grace.
